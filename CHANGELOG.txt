0.2.6
- adding a new actor now adds the actor to the middle of the current view, not the middle of the scene
    - so if you're zoomed into an area of a large map, adding an actor adds it where you can see into
- replaced simple tile name only dropdown with a searchable and scrollable tile selector in HUD settings
- fixed canvas clicking when an actor is selected adding another actor to the scene
- adds support for 16x16 and 32x32 tile imports
- adds a movement range visualization for actors and enemies
- when an actor is selected and has movement enabled, a colored overlay shows the movement range 
    - green overlay for horizontal movement
    - blue overlay for vertical movement
    - orange overlay for bounce movement
    - works for both actors and enemies (checks moveAmount and enemyRange properties)
    - visualization only appears when: 
        - using the actor or spawn tool
        - the actor is selected (activeActorId matches)
        - the actor has isMoving enabled
        - the movement range is greater than 0
